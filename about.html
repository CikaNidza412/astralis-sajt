About Astralis

Learn what Astralis publishes, how its examples are made, and what its interactive tools show.

What you can explore

Astralis publishes guides to Western astrology and the sky observations behind some of its vocabulary. The guides walk through topics such as reading a birth chart, interpreting a daily horoscope as a reflection prompt, and comparing the charts of two people.

You can read the guides and try the interactive tools without creating an account. Each tool is an illustration of an idea covered by the guides.

How we write the guides

Worked examples use hypothetical data and show the steps a reader can follow. We label illustrative placements and distinguish traditional astrological interpretations from astronomical observations. When a guide draws on external material, its source links accompany the article.

How to read an interactive result

The toolkit uses example placements and generated reflection prompts. It does not use astronomical ephemerides to calculate the historical sky. Use the pages to understand the layout of a chart, compare symbolic interpretations, or find a question worth reflecting on.
