Privacy policy

How Astralis handles browser storage, tool inputs, external services, and messages you send us.

Tool inputs and browser storage

Astralis runs the interactive examples in your browser. The site does not require an account. Your birth details and selected city are used by the tools on your device. The city choices come from a list included with the site.

Your language preference is stored in your browser. If you choose to save a profile, its details are stored in the same browser and may be seen by anyone who uses that browser profile. Use the clear option on the home page to remove your saved profile. You can also remove saved settings and profiles by clearing site data in your browser.

Site delivery and external links

Astralis is hosted on GitHub Pages and delivered through Cloudflare. These services may process request information such as your IP address, browser type, and the requested page to deliver the site, handle errors, and protect it from abuse. An external link takes you to another service that follows its own privacy policy.

Analytics and advertising

This version of Astralis has Google Analytics and Google advertising tags disabled. If analytics or ads are enabled, this notice will describe the services in use and the applicable consent controls. Analytics providers may process usage and device information to help measure how pages are used.

When Google AdSense is active, Google and other advertising partners may read or place cookies, or use web beacons, IP addresses, or similar identifiers to select, deliver, measure, and protect ads. Where the use of advertising or analytics technology requires permission, the site will present a consent choice. You can also manage personalized advertising through Google's My Ad Center.
