Terms of use

What to expect when you read Astralis guides or use its interactive examples.

Purpose of the site

Astralis offers educational material, interactive examples, and prompts for reflection. Astrological interpretations describe conventions used in a cultural tradition. The examples are simulations and do not establish predictions, probabilities, compatibility, or the actual positions of planets.

Using the examples

Use the explanations to understand how a chart is read. Base health, legal, financial, relationship, or other consequential decisions on relevant evidence and professional advice where appropriate. If you include someone else's details in a comparison, use them only with that person's permission. Fictional details are enough to try the tools.

Sources and changes

External sources have their own terms. A link offers further reading. If you spot an error or a link that no longer works, please tell us which page and passage need attention. We may revise examples, explanations, and links to improve the site.
